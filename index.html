Why VitaCalc works differently

Most health calculators exist to collect an email address. These exist to answer a question and get out of your way.

Nothing leaves your browser

Every formula runs locally in JavaScript. No account, no upload, no tracking of what you enter.

Answers without a page reload

Results appear instantly under the form, with the working shown so you can check it.

Built mobile-first

Large tap targets, numeric keypads on number fields and layouts that reflow to one column.

Formulas cited, not hidden

Each page names the equation it uses, the population it came from and where it breaks down.

Estimates, honestly labelled

Every result here is a population-level estimate, not a diagnosis. We show you the formula, the assumptions behind it and the situations where it stops being reliable — so you know exactly how much weight to give the number.
