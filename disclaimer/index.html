Health disclaimer

The calculators on this site provide general estimates for informational purposes only. They are not medical advice, not a diagnosis, and not a substitute for consultation with a qualified healthcare professional.

What an estimate means here

Every formula on this site was derived from measurements of a group of people and fitted to describe that group on average. Applied to one individual, it carries error — often more than people expect:

  • BMR prediction equations are typically within about 10% of lab-measured resting metabolism, meaning a 1,600 kcal estimate could plausibly be 1,440 or 1,760.
  • Circumference-based body fat estimates carry a standard error of roughly 3 to 4 percentage points against DEXA.
  • MET-based calorie estimates commonly differ from measured expenditure by 15 to 25% for an individual.
  • Around 4% of babies are born on their calculated due date; about 80% arrive within two weeks either side.
  • Ovulation timing varies between cycles even for people with regular periods, so predicted fertile windows are probabilities, not certainties.

Do not use these tools to

  • Diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease or condition.
  • Prevent pregnancy. Calendar-based cycle prediction is not a contraceptive method.
  • Make clinical decisions, including medication dosing, even though some formulas here originated in clinical dosing contexts.
  • Replace ultrasound dating or any assessment your clinician has made. Their date takes precedence over ours.
  • Justify extreme dieting, extreme training or any rapid change in body weight.

Please seek professional advice if

  • You are pregnant, trying to conceive, or breastfeeding.
  • You have diabetes, thyroid disease, heart or kidney disease, PCOS, an eating disorder, or any chronic condition.
  • You take medication that affects weight, appetite, fluid balance or metabolism.
  • You are planning a significant change to diet or exercise.
  • A result concerns you, or a symptom is troubling you — regardless of what any calculator says.
  • You are calculating on behalf of a child or teenager. Adult thresholds do not apply under 20.

Accuracy and liability

We implement published formulas carefully and verify them against reference values, but we make no warranty that results are accurate, complete or fit for any particular purpose. To the maximum extent permitted by law, VitaCalc accepts no liability for any loss or harm arising from use of, or reliance on, this website. Use it at your own discretion.

In an emergency

Do not use this website. Contact your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department immediately.
