Which one should I use?

A short guide to picking the right tool.

Checking your weight

Start with BMI for a quick screen, then body fat for what that weight is actually made of. Ideal weight adds clinical reference figures.

Managing calories

BMR gives your resting burn and daily maintenance calories. Calories burned covers what a specific session adds.

Training for a race

Pace solves for pace, finish time or distance and prints split times for every standard race distance.

Cycle and pregnancy

Period projects six cycles ahead. Pregnancy dates a due date and gestational age. Bra size converts across four sizing systems.
